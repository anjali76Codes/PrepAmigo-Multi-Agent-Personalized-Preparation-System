# Community code of conduct

PrepAmigo contributors and participants are expected to help create a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful when discussing ideas, code, and feedback.
- Focus on the work and its impact; critique proposals without attacking people.
- Share context, ask questions in good faith, and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, personal information, or private communications.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Include relevant context and links where possible. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy as reasonably possible, and take action appropriate to the circumstances, including removing content or restricting participation.
